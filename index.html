ABOUT THIS DISPLAY & ITS DATA

What is automatic? Overwatch console ranks are requested directly from the public OverFast API. This is a community service reading Blizzard's public career pages, not a live connection to the Xbox. PC ranks are never substituted.

What does the season mean? The displayed season is the identifier reported by the API for the last competitive season played. It is not independently confirmed as the active season, and the identifier may differ from the in-game label.

Why can a fresh lookup show older stats? Blizzard and the data provider cache updates. The profile-update time and our lookup time are separate. Rechecking cannot force Blizzard to publish a new rank.

How often? Once on opening if this browser has no recent result, then hourly while visible. No calls are made while the tab is hidden. A failed lookup may leave an explicitly labelled older copy for up to seven days. Refresh requests have a one-minute cooldown and respect provider retry delays.

Privacy and cost. This page has no visitor accounts, paid AI calls, advertising, or analytics code. It stores only this public player's last successful Overwatch response and request timing in your browser. Your browser contacts OverFast; the profile icon, if provided, loads from Blizzard's CDN. The hosting provider may keep its own access logs. No paid integrations are used.

Other games. Gears: E-Day and Diablo IV have no verified feed connected here. D2R is an external link only; we do not call D2R.ORG's API or copy its ladder data. D2R data-use terms ↗
