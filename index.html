ABOUT THIS DISPLAY & ITS DATA

What is automatic? Overwatch console ranks are requested directly from the public OverFast API. This is a community service reading Blizzard's public career pages, not a live connection to the Xbox. PC ranks are never substituted.

What does the season mean? The displayed season is the identifier reported by the API for the last competitive season played. It is not independently confirmed as the active season, and the identifier may differ from the in-game label.

Why can a fresh lookup show older stats? Blizzard and the data provider cache updates. The profile-update time and our lookup time are separate. Rechecking cannot force Blizzard to publish a new rank.

How often? Overwatch requests a new lookup whenever this page opens, subject to provider retry delays; it also rechecks hourly while visible. D2R requests our private Xbox backend when the page opens, within one shared free-tier budget. Nearby simultaneous visits may share a lookup, and service limits can require saved data. The display always keeps the actual successful-lookup timestamp. Saved data expires after seven days.

Own Xbox reader. Opening the page asks the owner’s local reader to inspect the Xbox game source in OBS. Two matching readings of the tested D2R character screen publish only character name, class, level and observation time. It never changes scenes, controls the game, or publishes screenshots/chat. Different games, missing stats and an offline reader retain the last saved character indefinitely with its original timestamp. Diablo IV uses the owner’s morning report instead; Gears details are coming soon.

Privacy and cost. This page has no visitor accounts, paid AI calls, advertising, or analytics code. It stores this public player's last successful game-stat responses and request timing in your browser. Your browser contacts OverFast and our private stats backend; the profile icon, if provided, loads from Blizzard's CDN. The Xbox key stays on the server and is never delivered to viewers. The hosting provider may keep its own access logs. No paid integrations are used.
